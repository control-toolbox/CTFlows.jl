**Version.** Last release `0.18.4`; last version in General `0.18.4`. Since `0.18.4`: breaking changes and new features. By `REL1` (0.Y.Z: the breaking component is Y, and a change breaks, so Y moves) and `REL2` (the triple must be above every one released: `0.18.4` is the last), the version is **`0.19.0`**.
**Jump in General (`REL3`).** none.
**Resolves from General alone (`REL4`).** ✅.
**Versions applied.** ct-agents `v0.5.0` (this recipe), ct-conventions `v0.15.0`, CTActions `dc60661` (the release tool).

### What changes since `0.18.4`

- Breaking: the multiplier of a labelled constraint is the dual of the solution, and `label => side` is removed (both were released in 0.18.3 and 0.18.4); the pseudo-Hamiltonian of a constrained flow is a `CTBase.Data.ConstrainedPseudoHamiltonian` that takes the multiplier as an argument, in both modes; `Systems.FrozenConstrainedPseudoHamiltonian` is removed, replaced by `CTBase.Data.freeze_multiplier`. Each has a migration block in `CHANGELOG.md` and `BREAKING.md`, run against 0.18.4 and the new version (#534, #531).
- New: `Flow(ocp)` and `Flow(ocp, law)` build their Hamiltonian from the CTModels getters and take the cost multiplier `p0`; state and control boxes are accepted by label in `constraint=`, and the solution carries the duals by label; the gradient getters of a constrained flow take the multiplier; `Systems.multiplier` is exported; batched states work with constraints.
- Improved: a `SolverFailure` from a flow call suggests `unsafe=true` (#523); a multiplier of the wrong dimension is rejected by label.
- Refactoring: `Systems.hamiltonian`, `pseudo_hamiltonian`, `control_law` and `multiplier` are the generic functions of `CTBase.Data`.
- Dependencies: the compat of `CTBase` moves to `0.30.15` and that of `CTModels` to `0.21.3`.
- Documentation: the page on `p0`, the dual convention, the internals of the duals (#535), the pin of ct-conventions v0.15.0.
- Added by the release PR: the entry of the removed export `Systems.FrozenConstrainedPseudoHamiltonian`, with its migration block. The other candidates listed by `facts` are one formatter run (#529), two documentation changes (#535) and two compat bumps, all covered.
- Open PRs, not in this release: none.
