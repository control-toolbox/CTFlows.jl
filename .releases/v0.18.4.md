- Fix: a multi-phase flow stops when a phase stops before its switching time; no jump is applied, the trajectory, its labels, switching times and grid end at the stop time, and the point call returns the state there (#526, #527).
- Tests and documentation for both; the pins of ct-conventions `v0.14.0` and ct-agents `v0.5.0`.
